An escalation has two halves — the ask and the answer — and both need a mechanism. Build the delivery path for the reply at the same time you build the queue that raises it. A verdict that reaches nobody is worse than an unanswered question: the decider believes they replied, and the next session re-asks. An answer once landed in a pane and nothing happened, because a rewrite moved replies into beads and silently dropped the notification leg with them.
