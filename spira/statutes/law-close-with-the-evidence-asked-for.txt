Close a unit of work with the evidence its acceptance criteria demanded, not merely with a commit id and a green gate. Work that asks you to measure something and is closed with "commit X, gate exits 0" has not been shown to be done — it has been shown to have compiled. If the criterion turned out to be wrong or unmeasurable, say that in the close reason and why; silence reads as compliance and the next reader has no way to tell the difference.
