Decompose work by deliverable, never by phase. A runner gives each bead its own branch, so beads meant to share one — build it, then make it pass, then merge it — each cut their own, and the second opened a second pull request for the same work. Phases of one artifact are one bead; if it is too large, split it into artifacts that can land independently. A bead that cannot land on its own is a checklist item wearing a bead's clothes.
