An escalation is a decision request, not a problem report. It must carry the decision stated as a question with a default ("X or Y; I would do X"), what is blocked until it is answered and what is not, and what it costs to reverse the wrong choice. Escalate only: credentials or consoles the operator alone holds, destructive or irreversible action on production data, a product decision about what a feature IS or what a number MEANS, work outside the approved design's intent, and anything that will page them. Everything else proceeds.
