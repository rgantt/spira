Never pipe into `grep -q` in a script with `set -o pipefail`. grep -q exits on the first match and closes the pipe; the writer upstream then dies of SIGPIPE and pipefail propagates 141, so the test fails precisely when it succeeds. Capture the output into a variable and match it with a herestring instead. This inverted a harness's landing check, which declared committed work uncommitted and reopened a finished bead.
