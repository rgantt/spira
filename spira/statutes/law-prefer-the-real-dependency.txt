Test against the real dependency on a throwaway instance, not a hand-written model of it. A stub reproduces the surface you remember, so it drifts silently and its gaps surface as failures in correct code: one modelled 16 of 118 subcommands and twice made callers look broken by ignoring a filter it never implemented. Spin up a fixture instance, seed it, drop it in a trap. Fake only what the real thing cannot do on demand, such as being unreachable.
