A branch belongs to the work, not to the bead id, so record it on the bead rather than deriving it. A derived name is a convention that breaks the moment work outlives the bead that began it: a successor bead cut its own branch for a deliverable already built and opened a second pull request for the same commits, splitting the review and doubling the CI. Recorded, the affinity survives a worker dying, a bead reopening, and work being handed on.
