A false alert is a real cost, not a harmless one. A check that false-failed for four days held its monitor in a failed state, and because a monitor already down does not alert again, a genuine outage produced no page: the noise CAUSED the silence. Never fix a noisy check by raising its threshold or widening a grace window — measure the property that actually matters rather than a proxy, and keep one check per failure mode. Signal an outage with silence, not a message.
