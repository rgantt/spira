CLOSED does not mean landed. A bead is closed when an agent says the work is done; it has landed when a commit on the repository's base branch carries it. Verify with `git merge-base --is-ancestor <commit> <base>`, never by comparing tip SHAs, because a tip moves under you mid-epic. Re-run `git fetch` immediately before asserting that anything did NOT land. Apply this after work finishes, never during — mid-flight, "not landed yet" and "never going to land" look identical.
