The public harness repository ships mechanism, never inventory. A statute, comment or script that names a failure mode of the machinery ships — those are precisely what a colleague would otherwise rediscover expensively. One that names a repository, a deploy path, a host's disks or the operator's own preferences stays private. The line is not scar-versus-clean: a scar stated generically is the most valuable thing shipped, while the same scar naming your infrastructure only teaches a colleague's agent to index on it.
