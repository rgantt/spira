Build the cheap check before the smart one. A harness that watches state must close the gap to its goal with deterministic predicates that run often and cost nothing, and reach for a model only when every rule has passed and the world is still wrong — that is the definition of judgement. A stall diagnosed by inference twice is a check nobody wrote. Inference is where a rule is discovered, never where it lives, and it must not act: the tier that costs the most should have the least authority.
