A guard must bind the actor who broke the rule, never the path or command the call travelled through. Before wrapping anything, grep every caller — including the tool's own plumbing. One shim cut two innocent callers: an escalation channel to the operator, and a submit command's notify step, which silently stranded four workers holding finished work. Identify the caller from argv[0] of the parent via /proc, never a command-line pattern — that matches your own arguments.
